First task - Kit purchase proof (mandatory):

  • Nothing else counts until this is accepted. Your other PRs will be closed until your Kit shows YES.
  • Photograph your board / invoice, upload it somewhere public (Google Drive / Imgur / LinkedIn post, "anyone can view"), copy the https://... link.
  • Copy submissions/TEMPLATE.json to submissions/<Your-Name>_PurchaseProof_<YYYY-MM-DD>.json (use your exact name as registered) and paste the photo link in the link field.
  • Open a PR titled [PurchaseProof] Your Name and paste the same link in the description.
  • Once merged, your Kit flips to YES on the scoreboard - you're unlocked.

How to submit your work:

Step 1 - Draft first: Prepare your work as a draft (Google Doc, public link) - do NOT post publicly yet.

Step 2 - Create file: One submission = one JSON file, like submissions/Arjun_A_Blog_2026-10-01.json

Step 3 - Open PR: Title like [Blog] Arjun A. Paste your draft link in the description also. One file = one PR.

Step 4 - Wait: We check it. If correct, we Merge it.

Step 5 - Post: Only AFTER merge, post the final version on the actual platform (Medium / x.com / LinkedIn / GitHub / YouTube).

Step 6 - Track: Watch your score on the live leaderboard in the repo README (SCORES.md has the full table).

REMEMBER: PROOF FIRST, THEN PR, THEN POST. POST BEFORE MERGE = NO POINTS.

Good luck, and see you in the groups!
